Turn a Yoga Class Video Into a 30-Second iPhone Timelapse

Want to turn a full yoga class recording into a 30-second video? Set the length you want and speed up the whole session evenly, instead of cutting out just the first 30 seconds. I made the iPhone app Yoga Glow (瑜光 30) for that: pick a video, choose 15, 30, or 60 seconds, and save the result to Photos. Processing happens on your phone, and the original stays untouched. This article explains how to use it, how it differs from iMovie and Camera timelapse, and what its yoga timer does. I’ll also cover what the app doesn’t do.

1. The options for shortening a long practice video on iPhone

Many people set their phone beside the mat and record a whole yoga class. Afterward, they might review their poses or want a short video of the session. Here are common ways to turn an hour of footage into a few seconds:

In iMovie, you use a slider to set the speed. Apple’s instructions don’t describe an option to specify the finished length in seconds, so you need to calculate the speed yourself to get exactly 30 seconds. With Camera’s Time-Lapse mode, you make that choice before recording. To review a pose at normal speed, you would need a separate recording. Account, upload, and privacy rules vary across editing apps, so check an app’s privacy information before using it.

Yoga Glow does one thing: it speeds up your entire selected practice range evenly to the length you choose and exports a silent video.

2. How to use Yoga Glow: Pick a video, set the length, and save

The app opens on the “Create” tab. You don’t need to sign up:

  1. Pick a video: Tap “Choose Video from Photos” or “Import from Files.” If you don’t have a practice recording yet, tap “Try a Sample Video” to walk through the steps with the included clip.
  2. Set the pace: Choose “Full Timelapse” as the mode, then choose 15, 30, or 60 seconds under “Video Length.” Use the “Start” and “End” sliders to leave out the part where you set up your phone or put away your mat. Tap “Reset Range” to use the whole video again.
  3. Create: Tap “Create My Video.” You’ll see the progress while it runs, and you can tap “Cancel” to stop.
  4. Save it: Tap “Play Preview” to watch the result, then save it to “Photos” or use the share button.

Your finished videos also stay in “My Videos” in the app, where you can play, share, or delete them. Your original long video won’t be changed or deleted.

If the original is stored only in iCloud Photos, the system downloads it to your phone before processing begins. Keep the app in the foreground while it works. The screen stays on during processing.

3. Timelapse details: Speed, source length, and quality

  • The whole range, not just the beginning: “Full Timelapse” samples frames evenly from the range you select. Your warm-up, standing poses, twists, and final rest can all appear in the short video. The settings show the speed and finished length. For example, 60 minutes shortened to 30 seconds is 120x speed.
  • Want one section at normal speed? Choose “Extract Clip” instead. It takes the number of seconds you select starting at your chosen point, without changing the speed.
  • If the original is shorter: If your video is shorter than the length you choose, the app exports it at its original length. It won’t slow it down to fill the time.
  • Export format: A silent MP4. It keeps the original aspect ratio and portrait or landscape orientation without cropping. There’s no audio track, so background noise and breathing won’t be in the short video.
  • Quality: You can choose 720p or 1080p for free. With Yoga Glow Pro, you can choose “Original Quality” to keep the source resolution. If you recorded in 4K, the export is 4K. The file will be larger and take longer to make. The app won’t upscale beyond the original video’s quality.

All processing happens on your phone, so long videos use its computing resources and storage. The app checks available space first and warns you if there isn’t enough, rather than leaving a half-finished file. Imported videos and finished clips both take up space on your phone. You can delete clips you no longer need from “My Videos.”

4. The built-in yoga timer: Sound cues and stillness count

The “Timer” tab has a timer for practice on the mat. I designed it so you don’t have to keep looking at the screen. It makes a sound when you need a cue.

  • Choose a time: Swipe left or right to choose 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, or 60 minutes, then tap to start. The last card, “Custom,” lets you choose 1 to 180 minutes. Tapping another duration during practice starts a new pose.
  • Sound cues: You hear one sound with 30 seconds left, two with 10 seconds left, and three with 5 seconds left. A bell rings when time is up, and your phone vibrates. For a 5-minute timer, there’s also a two-tone cue at 3 minutes and 1 minute remaining.
  • After the bell: Large numbers count up from 1 to show how long you stay still. The color changes over time: green for the first minute, then blue, and purple after five minutes.
  • Your session: “This Session” at the top starts counting when you first tap a duration. Switching poses doesn’t reset it. Tap “End” to finish and reset it. You can also “Pause” and “Resume” during practice.
  • Cues without watching your phone: The screen stays on while the timer runs. A Live Activity shows the current time and status on the Lock Screen. If the app isn’t in the foreground, a notification and bell alert you when time is up. To use these features, allow notifications when first asked and keep Live Activities on.

The large numbers in the app and on the Lock Screen show how long the current pose has been going, not a countdown. The outer ring gradually fills. The timer is free, and there are no ads on this tab.

5. Free version, ads, and Yoga Glow Pro

Yoga Glow is free to download and supported by ads:

  • Free version: There’s a banner ad at the bottom of the Create screen. To save a video to Photos, tap “Watch Ad to Save.” It saves after you watch an ad. If no ad is available, you can still save your video.
  • Yoga Glow Pro: This is a one-time purchase with no monthly fee. It removes ads and unlocks “Original Quality” exports, including 4K. The price is NT$30 in Taiwan; check the App Store for your price. If you change phones or reinstall the app, tap “Restore Purchases.”

Google AdMob provides the ads. When you first open the app, iOS asks whether to allow tracking. You can decline and still use every feature. You’ll see general ads instead. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and usage data. Details are in the privacy policy. Your practice videos and finished clips are not uploaded.

6. What it doesn’t do

  1. No editing or extras: There are no filters, beauty effects, captions, music, or speed changes for separate sections. If you need those, you can make a 30-second video in Yoga Glow first, then edit it in your usual video or social app.
  2. No audio: Exports are always silent. Use another tool if you want to keep your teacher’s instructions or music.
  3. iPhone only: The app requires iOS 18 or later. Processing speed depends on your phone and the length of your video. I haven’t timed it on every phone, so I won’t give a number of seconds here.

7. Frequently asked questions

Does Yoga Glow upload my practice video?

No. The video you choose and the clip you make are processed on your iPhone. They aren’t uploaded, and you don’t need an account. If the original is stored only in iCloud Photos, the system downloads it to your phone first. Google AdMob provides the in-app ads and collects ad-related data, but it doesn’t get your videos.

Can I use it without internet access?

Yes. Video processing happens on your phone and doesn’t need internet access. Ads need internet access to load. If no ad is available, “Watch Ad to Save” still saves your video.

My video is only 20 seconds. What happens if I choose 30 seconds?

It exports at its original 20-second length. It won’t slow down to fill the time. The settings warn you if the original is shorter than the length you choose.

Can I see the timer on the Lock Screen?

Yes. Once you start the timer, a Live Activity on the Lock Screen shows how long the current pose has been going. After the bell, it shows how many seconds you’ve stayed still. If the app isn’t in the foreground, a notification and bell alert you when time is up. You need to allow notifications.

What phone do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 18 or later.
