Why Yoga Glow

Whole-session timelapse

Evenly speeds up the full class into 15, 30 or 60 seconds — keeps the arc, not just the start.

On-device, private

Your video is processed on your phone. No account, no upload, works offline.

Keep the original

Exports a silent MP4 in the original aspect; your source video is never changed.

Yoga Timer

A free practice timer built in — gold, calm, and designed to speak only when it should.

  • 1–30 minutes — swipe left or right to choose
  • The language of sound: one beep at 30s left, two at 10s, three at 5s, a single bell at the end
  • After the bell, a still count rises — green, then blue, then purple
  • Shows the countdown on your Lock Screen, so you can glance without unlocking
  • Total practice time keeps running from your first tap
