Does it upload my video?No. The video you pick and the clip you make are processed entirely on your iPhone and never leave the device.
Why does it ask about tracking?Yoga Glow shows ads to stay free. iOS asks once whether to allow tracking; you can decline and still use everything, with non-personalized ads.
Where are my clips?Saved inside the app, and to Photos when you tap Save. Open the history button (top-right) to see past clips.
